How we got here

Eight chapters, from a KT session to today. Scroll slowly.

Chapter 1 of 8May 2025 · Coal India project

The KT session

You joined Accenture as my junior, and I was the one assigned to give you KT. I had a plan for every session. I had no plan for you. Somewhere in those first weeks, you started liking me. Read the last line of the code on the screen.

Chapter 2 of 8After the KT

One ABAP object at a time

Reports, interfaces, conversions, enhancements, forms and workflows. Whenever a new object came your way, I helped you with it. Every call and every screen share quietly became the best part of my day.

Chapter 3 of 813 June 2026 · Ranchi

The first time we met

The Coal India project team went to Ranchi to celebrate, and you came too. Until then we had only seen each other on screens. That evening we sat by the pool, just the two of us, and talked and talked.

Chapter 4 of 8After the party

More than 1,000 km, never silent

From that night we talked the whole day. You went back to Nagpur and I went back to Delhi, and a video call became our way of sharing a room, with kisses through the screen.

Chapter 5 of 821 August 2026

Delhi to Nagpur, the first time

I took my first flight to you. We stayed together, it was romantic, and we went out and explored side by side. The days we had only talked about were finally real.

Chapter 6 of 8Back in Delhi

The forgotten laptop

Back home I realised I had left my laptop at the airport. An ABAP developer without a laptop. So I flew to Nagpur again to collect it, and you scolded me properly. It was also one more trip to you, so I did not mind.

Chapter 7 of 819 September 2026

The third trip

I came to Nagpur once more, and we enjoyed every single hour. Then I flew back to Delhi, and the countdown to the next time began.

Chapter 8 of 8Now

Elephant and Elephant

Our nickname is an elephant with a heart, and we call each other baby. We still talk every day, and neither of us can stay without the other. Tap anywhere around the elephants.
